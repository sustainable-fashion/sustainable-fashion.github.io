How can the fashion and textile industry move from linear production to circular systems? This interdisciplinary workshop examines approaches to closing material loops through design principles, life cycle assessment, and digital technologies that support circularity, transparency, and traceability.

It brings together perspectives from research and practice to consider how circular strategies can be implemented across the value chain, from product design and assessment to data-driven solutions and organisational change.

About

From linear production to circular systems.

The programme connects fashion design, life cycle assessment, and information systems, with contributions from researchers and practitioners working across these areas.

Thread 01

Circular Fashion

Design approaches to modularity, reuse, and remanufacturing are considered through prototypes, garment collections, and lived practice.

Thread 02

Life Cycle Assessment

Life Cycle Assessment helps organizations evaluate the environmental implications of circular strategies across materials, products, and systems, becoming vital during the transition to a sustainable CE.

Thread 03

Information Systems

Digital Product Passports and related information systems are examined as infrastructures for product data, traceability, and circular value creation.

Circular Lab

Part of a wider research initiative.

This workshop is part of Circular Lab.

The Circular Lab is an interdisciplinary research and practice network dedicated to advancing the circular economy in the Lake Constance region. As a central hub for circular innovation and business-oriented sustainability, the Lab collaborates with companies, universities, and other stakeholders to develop practical approaches for implementing circular value creation, particularly in the textile industry and the agricultural and food sectors.

The programme

Research and practice collaboration

From June 2023 to May 2027, the Circular Lab will be funded by the Interreg VI "Alpenrhein-Bodensee-Hochrhein" (ABH) programme, whose resources are provided by the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) and the Swiss Confederation.

Two focus areas

Textiles · Agri-food

The Lab develops practical approaches for circular value creation, particularly in the textiles and agri-food sectors. This workshop contributes to its textiles focus area.

The work

From strategies to implementation

Circular Lab focuses on circular products and business models, supports cross-company ecosystems, and formulates strategies and recommendations that advance ecological and economic sustainability.
